Five commands

hunch test  world.hunch    compile it, show what each agent sees, check its asserts
hunch train world.hunch    fill the blanks, and say what was found
hunch run   world.hunch    one episode, step by step
hunch why   world.hunch    what every other action would have been worth
hunch play  world.hunch    take a seat and play against it

train saves the policy beside the world, and the others pick it up from there. Two more commands are for evaluation: hunch score prints a saved policy's report again, and hunch grade scores an outside agent, such as a script or a language model.
